Times of Crypto Era — Redesign Directions

Five distinct products, not five reskins — each has its own module set, its own layout logic, and its own extra features (not just recolored copies of the same page).

Five directions

Meridian

Financial-authority desk. No card grid — dense editorial rows, like a real financial paper.
Fear & Greed gauge "Morning Ledger" email band Snapshot mini-table Social in utility bar

Volt

Consumer fintech app feel. No plain ticker bar — coin cards with live sparklines instead.
Sparkline coin cards Horizontal trending chips Email CTA embedded in the grid Social buttons in header

Obsidian

Pro-trader dark terminal. Data-first sidebar, dense list instead of a card grid.
Gainers / Losers table Inline Fear & Greed readout Telegram "Alpha Channel" CTA Dense list, no thumbnails

Ember

Warm editorial masthead. No live ticker at all — this outlet leads with journalism, not the tape.
Editor's Picks (curated, distinct from Trending) Newsletter sidebar box Asymmetric newspaper grid "Follow our coverage" band

Pulse

Bold social-native crypto brand. Sidebar is live stats, not more articles.
Trending on X (social commentary cards) Crypto Calendar (FOMC, unlocks, ETF dates) Learn the Basics row Bold gradient email banner
