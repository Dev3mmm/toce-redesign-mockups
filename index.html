Simple, easy-to-navigate homepages — classic top-to-bottom flow, everything scannable. All three carry the same feature set (date, Fear & Greed, heatmap, movers, ad slot, guides, email signup, latest grid); the difference is genuine visual identity, not structure.

Warm cream paper, serif headlines, classic newspaper dateline and masthead. Print-inspired trust.
