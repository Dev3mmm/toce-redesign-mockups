Times of Crypto Era — Redesign Directions

Each of these leads with a different anchor concept — not a hero story with a sidebar recolored, an actually different front door.

New: different front-door concepts

Atlas

Anchor: live market heatmap Opens on a color-coded heatmap of the top 24 coins by cap — data visualization as the front door, not a story. Plus a daily crypto-term glossary card.

Roundtable

Anchor: people & media Watch & Listen row (podcast/video), an author-spotlight strip naming the actual reporters, and a live reader poll (Bullish/Bearish). Personality-led, not algorithm-led.

Ledger Sheet

Anchor: comparison table + live blog Opens on a BTC/ETH/SOL spreadsheet-style compare table, then a "Today in Crypto" chronological live-blog timeline instead of a trending list. Data-desk feel.

Compass

Anchor: browse by topic, not by "latest" No single lead story up top — big colored topic tiles (Bitcoin/Ethereum/Solana/DeFi/Regulation) are the front door. Explore-first navigation for readers who came for a topic, not a headline.
