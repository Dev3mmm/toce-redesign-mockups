Times of Crypto Era — Redesign Directions

Five distinct brand executions of the same real crypto-news layout (ticker, breaking badge, trending rail, market movers, story grid) — like five competing outlets, not five art projects.

Five brand directions

Meridian

Financial-authority. Navy on warm paper, deep gold accent, slab-serif headlines. Bloomberg/WSJ-adjacent gravitas.

Volt

Consumer fintech energy. White ground, electric blue, rounded pills. Coinbase/Robinhood-adjacent.

Obsidian

Premium dark minimalism. Near-black, crisp white type, muted emerald accent. The Block-adjacent pro feel.

Ember

Warm editorial authority. Cream paper, burnt-orange masthead, classic serif. CoinDesk-adjacent.

Pulse

Bold crypto-native energy. Deep violet ground, vivid magenta accent, condensed display type. Decrypt-adjacent.
